## How to use this
Useful even as a solo developer: it's the vocabulary for deciding what to build next and how to know if it worked, not just how to build it.

## Suggested order if starting from zero
1. **Product-Market Fit → MVP (Minimum Viable Product)** — the earliest-stage concepts, what you're actually trying to prove
2. **User Story → Product Roadmap → Prioritization Frameworks** — how work gets defined and sequenced
3. **North Star Metric → OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) → A/B Testing** — how you measure whether any of it is working
