# Product Roadmap

**Definition:** A high-level plan communicating what a product team intends to build, and roughly when, organized around goals rather than a fixed, detailed schedule.

## How It Works
- Good roadmaps organize around themes or outcomes ("improve onboarding") rather than a rigid list of exact features and dates
- Time horizons are usually looser the further out they go, "this quarter" is specific, "next year" is directional
- Serves two different audiences at once: internal teams need enough detail to plan, external stakeholders need enough clarity without an overly binding commitment

## Why It Matters
- Aligns engineering, design, sales, and leadership around the same set of priorities instead of everyone pulling in a different direction

## Common Pitfalls
- Publishing a roadmap as a fixed set of promises with hard dates, then facing real backlash when priorities inevitably shift
- Organizing a roadmap purely around features instead of the problems those features are meant to solve, losing the "why" behind each item

## Example
A quarterly roadmap lists "reduce checkout drop-off" as a theme, with the specific features to address it left flexible as the team learns more.
