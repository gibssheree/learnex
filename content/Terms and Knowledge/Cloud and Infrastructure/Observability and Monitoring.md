# Observability and Monitoring

**Definition:** The ability to infer the internal operating state of distributed cloud applications by analyzing their Telemetry data: Metrics, Logs, and Traces (The 3 Pillars of Observability).

## How It Works
- Metrics: numeric aggregated time-series counters and gauges measuring health (Prometheus, Datadog)
- Logs: detailed timestamped text events generated by applications
- Traces: distributed trace spans tracking single request execution paths across multiple microservices (OpenTelemetry, Jaeger)

## Why It Matters
- Critical for rapid debugging of outages, performance bottlenecks, and latency degradation in complex microservice meshes

## Common Pitfalls
- Relying solely on system metrics without distributed tracing when debugging microservice latency spikes
