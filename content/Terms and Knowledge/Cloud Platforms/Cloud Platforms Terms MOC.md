## How to use this
Use this to pick a platform, not just learn a concept. Cross-reference the "Core Services & Concepts" section of each note against the concept folders elsewhere in the vault to see how the same idea, serverless, CDN, IAM, is implemented differently across vendors.

## Suggested order if starting from zero
1. **AWS** — the default most tutorials and job listings assume
2. **Vercel or Netlify** — if you're doing frontend work, the easiest real deploy experience
3. **Cloudflare** — understand edge computing and CDN from the company that popularized it for developers
4. **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** and **Microsoft Azure** — once you know AWS's shape, these are fast to compare against it
5. **Railway, Render, DigitalOcean, Fly.io** — smaller platforms, good for side projects, compare pricing and simplicity tradeoffs
6. **Akamai** and **Fastly** — enterprise CDN landscape, useful context even if you won't touch them day to day
