# Railway

**Definition:** A modern, developer-friendly platform for deploying full-stack apps and databases with minimal configuration.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **Container deploys** — [[Docker]], deploys straight from a Dockerfile or an auto-detected buildpack
- **Managed Postgres/Redis/MySQL** — [[Database Migration]], one-click managed databases
- **Environment-based deploys** — [[Environment Variables]]

## Pros
- Extremely simple setup compared to AWS
- Genuinely usable free/hobby tier
- Good fit for full-stack apps that need a real backend plus a database

## Cons
- Smaller scale ceiling than the hyperscalers
- Less mature enterprise features like compliance and advanced networking

## Best For
- Side projects and small startups needing a backend and database without managing infrastructure

## Real Examples
- Widely used by indie developers and early-stage startups for MVPs

## Use Cases
- Full-stack app hosting
- Side projects
- Small SaaS backends
