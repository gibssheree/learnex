# Akamai

**Definition:** One of the original CDN companies, dating back to 1998, now also offering cloud computing after acquiring Linode.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **CDN** — [[Content Delivery Network (CDN) and Edge Computing]], the oldest and one of the largest CDN networks in the world
- **Kona Site Defender (WAF)** — [[OWASP Top 10 Security Risks]]
- **EdgeWorkers** — [[Serverless Computing and Cold Starts]], edge compute similar to Cloudflare Workers
- **Linode (Akamai Cloud Compute)** — [[Virtual Machines (VMs)]], simple, developer-friendly VM hosting acquired in 2022

## Pros
- Deep enterprise relationships and massive network reach built over 25+ years
- Strong in media streaming and large file delivery at scale

## Cons
- Less developer mindshare than Cloudflare
- Enterprise-first pricing and sales process makes it less approachable for small projects

## Best For
- Large media and streaming companies
- Enterprises needing established, contract-based CDN relationships

## Real Examples
- Used by many major broadcasters and large e-commerce sites for video delivery and traffic surges

## Use Cases
- Video streaming delivery
- E-commerce flash-sale traffic spikes
- Enterprise web security
