# DigitalOcean

**Definition:** A cloud provider focused on simplicity and predictable pricing, popular with individual developers and small teams since before AWS/GCP/Azure targeted that audience.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **Droplets** — [[Virtual Machines (VMs)]], DigitalOcean's famously simple, flat-rate VM product
- **DOKS** — [[Kubernetes (K8s)]], managed Kubernetes
- **Spaces** — [[Cloud Storage Systems]], S3-compatible object storage
- **App Platform** — [[CI-CD|CI/CD]], PaaS-style deploys similar to Heroku or Render

## Pros
- Simple, predictable flat-rate pricing
- Excellent documentation and community tutorials, widely considered some of the best in the industry
- Low learning curve

## Cons
- Smaller service catalog than the hyperscalers
- Fewer regions
- Less suited for very large enterprise workloads

## Best For
- Individual developers and small startups
- Learning cloud infrastructure without AWS's complexity

## Real Examples
- Popular among indie hackers and bootstrapped startups for VPS hosting

## Use Cases
- Personal projects
- Small business websites
- Learning Linux server administration
