# Computer Vision

**Definition:** The field of AI focused on enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information from images or video.

## How It Works
- Classical: hand-crafted feature detectors (edges, corners)
- Modern: CNNs and Vision Transformers learn hierarchical features directly from labeled image data

## Why It Matters
- Powers facial recognition, medical imaging diagnosis, self-driving perception, and photo search
- One of the earliest deep-learning success stories (ImageNet breakthroughs, 2012 onward)

## Common Pitfalls
- Training on unrepresentative image datasets, causing poor real-world generalization (lighting, angles, demographics)
- Confusing detection accuracy on benchmarks with robustness in messy, real-world conditions

- [[Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)]]

## Example
A phone unlocking via face recognition, or a self-driving car identifying pedestrians and traffic signs, both rely on computer vision.
