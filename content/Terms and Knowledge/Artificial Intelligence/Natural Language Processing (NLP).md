# Natural Language Processing (NLP)

**Definition:** The field of AI focused on enabling machines to understand, generate, and work with human language.

## How It Works
- Classical pipeline: tokenize → tag parts of speech → parse → extract meaning
- Modern approach: feed text (as tokens/embeddings) into a transformer model trained on massive corpora

## Why It Matters
- Powers search, translation, chatbots, sentiment analysis, and virtually all LLM applications
- The field that LLMs like GPT-class models grew out of

## Common Pitfalls
- Underestimating ambiguity — the same sentence can have multiple valid parses/meanings
- Ignoring language/cultural bias baked into training corpora
