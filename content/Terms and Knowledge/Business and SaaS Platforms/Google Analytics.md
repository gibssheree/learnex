# Google Analytics

**Definition:** The most widely used web analytics platform, tracking visitor behavior, traffic sources, and conversions on websites and apps.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **Event tracking** — [[Event-Driven Architecture]]
- **REST API** — [[REST API]]

## Pros
- Free for most use cases
- Massive documentation and community
- Deep integration with Google Ads

## Cons
- GA4's interface and data model were a major, widely-criticized overhaul from the older Universal Analytics
- Privacy regulations like GDPR complicate its use in some regions

## Best For
- Any website or app wanting free, comprehensive traffic and behavior analytics

## Real Examples
- Used on the vast majority of public websites

## Use Cases
- Website traffic analysis
- Conversion tracking
- Marketing campaign attribution
