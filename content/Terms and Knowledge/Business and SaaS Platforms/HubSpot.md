# HubSpot

**Definition:** A CRM platform built around inbound marketing, bundling CRM, email marketing, landing pages, and website tools into one product organized as separate "Hubs" (Marketing, Sales, Service, CMS, Operations). Popular with small-to-mid-size businesses.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **REST API** — [[REST API]], object-based endpoints (contacts, companies, deals, tickets) plus a CRM object association model for linking records across Hubs
- **Workflows** — [[Webhook]], visual automation builder whose triggers can enroll records based on property changes, form submissions, or events, and can call external webhooks or run custom code actions
- **HubDB & CMS Hub** — a lightweight structured-data table plus website/CMS tooling for building marketing sites on the same platform as the CRM data
- **Free CRM tier** — the core CRM is free indefinitely; monetization comes from paid Marketing, Sales, Service, and Operations Hub add-ons, each sold in Starter/Professional/Enterprise tiers
- **Seats vs. marketing contacts pricing** — Sales/Service Hub bills per seat, Marketing Hub bills by number of "marketing contacts" in your database, a pricing axis that catches growing companies off guard

## Pros
- Much easier to set up and administer than Salesforce, minimal admin overhead for a small team
- Generous free tier that includes core CRM, forms, and basic email
- Strong marketing automation and lead-scoring built in without needing a separate marketing automation platform

## Cons
- Costs scale up quickly once advanced features (custom reporting, more workflows, more marketing contacts) are needed — Enterprise tiers run into the thousands per month
- Less deep customization than Salesforce for complex, multi-branch enterprise sales processes
- Contact-based pricing can spike unexpectedly as an email list grows even if sales usage stays flat

## Best For
- Small-to-mid-size businesses wanting CRM plus inbound marketing in one connected tool rather than stitching together separate systems

## Real Examples
- Widely used by startups and marketing-led B2B companies as their combined CRM and marketing automation platform

## Use Cases
- Inbound marketing and lead nurturing
- Small sales team pipeline tracking
- Email marketing campaigns and landing page hosting
