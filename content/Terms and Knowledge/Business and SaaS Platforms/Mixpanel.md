# Mixpanel

**Definition:** A product analytics platform focused on user behavior and event tracking inside applications, more product-focused than Google Analytics' traffic focus.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **Event tracking** — [[Event-Driven Architecture]]
- **REST API** — [[REST API]]

## Pros
- Strong for tracking in-product user behavior and funnels
- Better suited to SaaS product analytics than GA's web-traffic focus

## Cons
- More expensive than Google Analytics at scale
- Requires more deliberate event-tracking setup to get value from

## Best For
- SaaS products wanting to understand user behavior and feature adoption inside the app itself

## Real Examples
- Used by many SaaS companies to track feature adoption and user funnels

## Use Cases
- Product analytics
- Conversion funnel analysis
- Feature adoption tracking
