# Zoho CRM

**Definition:** A budget-friendly CRM from Zoho's larger suite of 40+ business applications, popular with small businesses wanting an all-in-one, affordable toolset rather than best-of-breed point solutions.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **REST API** — [[REST API]], plus Deluge, Zoho's own lightweight scripting language for custom workflow logic, similar in spirit to Salesforce's [[Apex]] but far simpler
- **Zoho ecosystem** — integrates tightly with Zoho's own suite (Books for accounting, Desk for support, Campaigns for email) rather than relying heavily on third-party integrations, so value compounds the more of the suite you adopt
- **Zia (AI assistant)** — built-in predictive lead scoring, anomaly detection, and conversational query features bundled into higher tiers rather than sold separately
- **Blueprint** — a visual process builder for enforcing multi-stage approval workflows (e.g. a deal can't move to "Closed Won" without a manager's sign-off)

## Pros
- Very affordable compared to Salesforce or HubSpot — paid tiers commonly start under $20/user/month, a fraction of comparable Salesforce editions
- Wide feature set for the price, including AI features (Zia) that competitors charge extra for
- Part of a broader affordable business-software suite, so a small company can run CRM, accounting, and support on one vendor's billing

## Cons
- Interface feels dated compared to Pipedrive or HubSpot's more polished UX
- Smaller third-party integration ecosystem than Salesforce, so anything outside Zoho's own suite may need custom API work
- Support and documentation quality is noticeably weaker at lower price tiers

## Best For
- Small businesses and budget-conscious teams wanting an all-in-one suite instead of paying separately for CRM, accounting, and support tools

## Real Examples
- Popular among small businesses and solo entrepreneurs globally, especially outside the US where Zoho's pricing has a stronger relative advantage

## Use Cases
- Small business sales tracking
- Budget-conscious CRM adoption
- Combined CRM plus back-office (invoicing, support) on a single vendor
