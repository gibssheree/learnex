# Zapier

**Definition:** A no-code automation platform that connects thousands of apps together, triggering actions in one app based on events in another.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **Webhooks** — [[Webhook]], the underlying mechanism most Zapier integrations are built on
- **REST API** — [[REST API]]
- **Trigger-action model** — [[Event-Driven Architecture]]

## Pros
- Connects an enormous number of apps without writing code
- Huge time-saver for repetitive cross-app workflows

## Cons
- Can get expensive at high task volumes
- Complex multi-step automations become hard to debug
- Adds latency compared to a direct API integration

## Best For
- Non-developers and small teams automating repetitive tasks across multiple tools

## Real Examples
- Widely used by small businesses and solo founders to glue together CRM, email, and spreadsheet tools

## Use Cases
- Cross-app automation, e.g. a new form submission creates a CRM lead and sends a Slack notification
- No-code workflow building
