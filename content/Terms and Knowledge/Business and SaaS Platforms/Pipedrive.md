# Pipedrive

**Definition:** A CRM built specifically around visual, Kanban-style sales-pipeline management, designed by salespeople to be simple for sales teams rather than a full business suite covering marketing and support.

## Core Services & Concepts
- **REST API** — [[REST API]], plus a v2 API redesign focused on faster field-level filtering for larger deal volumes
- **Webhooks** — [[Webhook]], fire on deal stage changes, new activities, or won/lost outcomes for syncing into other systems
- **Activities-based selling** — the product is opinionated around always attaching a next scheduled activity (call, email, meeting) to a deal, nudging reps to keep momentum rather than letting deals go stale
- **Deal rotting/stage probability** — deals visually "rot" (get flagged) if left untouched past a configurable threshold, and each stage carries a default win-probability weight for forecasting

## Pros
- Extremely simple, visual pipeline interface that most reps learn in under an hour
- Fast to onboard a sales team onto, minimal admin configuration required to get value
- Affordable mid-tier pricing compared to Salesforce, with most core sales features available below the top tier

## Cons
- Fewer marketing/support features than HubSpot or Salesforce, usually needs to be paired with separate marketing and support tools
- Less suited outside of pure sales use cases; weak for complex multi-department approval workflows
- Reporting and customization get noticeably shallower than Salesforce once a team's process gets complex

## Best For
- Small sales teams wanting a focused, easy-to-use pipeline tool without the configuration overhead of a full CRM suite

## Real Examples
- Popular among small-to-mid B2B sales teams, especially outbound-heavy teams that value activity tracking over marketing integration

## Use Cases
- Visual deal pipeline tracking
- Small team sales processes
- Activity-driven outbound sales tracking
