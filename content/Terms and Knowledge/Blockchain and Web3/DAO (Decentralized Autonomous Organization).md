# DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)

**Definition:** An organization governed by rules encoded in smart contracts and member voting, rather than a traditional hierarchy of executives and a board.

## How It Works
- Membership and voting power are usually tied to holding a specific token
- Proposals (spend funds, change a protocol parameter) are voted on by members, and approved proposals execute automatically via [[Smart Contract]] logic
- Treasury funds are typically held in a shared wallet controlled by the DAO's voting process, not any single individual

## Why It Matters
- Represents an attempt to run collective decision-making and fund management transparently on-chain instead of through a traditional legal corporate structure

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming token-based voting is automatically fair, wealth concentration in a few large token holders can dominate outcomes just like traditional shareholder voting
- Underestimating governance attack risk, a DAO's own voting mechanism can sometimes be exploited to pass malicious proposals

## Example
A DAO might collectively vote on how to allocate a shared treasury of pooled cryptocurrency toward funding open-source development.
