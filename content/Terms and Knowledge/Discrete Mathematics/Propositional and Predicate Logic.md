# Propositional and Predicate Logic

**Definition:** Formal mathematical systems for constructing rigorous statements using logical operators (AND, OR, NOT, Implication) and quantifiers (Universal ∀, Existential ∃).

## How It Works
- Propositional Logic: evaluates atomic statements that are strictly True or False
- Predicate Logic: extends propositional logic with predicates and quantifiers: Universal (`∀x P(x)`) and Existential (`∃x P(x)`)
- Truth Tables: evaluates boolean validity of compound logical expressions

## Why It Matters
- Forms the mathematical foundation for boolean algebra, hardware digital logic gates, program conditionals, and formal software verification

## Common Pitfalls
- Confusing logical implication (`P -> Q`) with causality, or misapplying De Morgan's Laws when negating quantified predicates
