# Proof Techniques

**Definition:** Structured mathematical methods used to establish the unassailable truth of logical propositions.

## How It Works
- Direct Proof: assumes P is true and logically derives Q
- Proof by Contradiction: assumes statement is False and shows it leads to an impossible logical contradiction
- Proof by Mathematical Induction: 1) Base Case P(1), 2) Inductive Hypothesis P(k), 3) Prove P(k+1)

## Why It Matters
- Crucial for proving algorithm correctness, loop invariants, and asymptotic Big-O bounds

## Common Pitfalls
- Failing to prove base case in mathematical induction proofs, invalidating the entire inductive chain

Proving √2 is irrational using Proof by Contradiction.
