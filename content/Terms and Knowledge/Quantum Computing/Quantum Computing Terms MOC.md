## How to use this
Core Concepts explain what a quantum computer actually is, physically and mathematically. Algorithms & Circuits explain what you can actually do with one, and why cryptographers care. [[Shor's Algorithm]] is the direct link to [[Symmetric and Asymmetric Encryption]] in [[Cybersecurity Terms MOC|Cybersecurity]] — read that connection, it's the most concrete real-world stake in this whole field right now.

## Suggested order if starting from zero
1. **Qubit → Superposition → Entanglement** — the three ideas everything else depends on
2. **Quantum Decoherence** — why quantum computers are so hard to actually build
3. **Quantum Gate** — how a qubit's state actually gets manipulated into a computation
4. **Shor's Algorithm → Grover's Algorithm** — the two most famous quantum algorithms, and why one is a much bigger deal than the other
5. **Quantum Supremacy** — the milestone marker, makes most sense once everything above already clicks
