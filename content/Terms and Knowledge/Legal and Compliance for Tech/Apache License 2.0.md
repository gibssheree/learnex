# Apache License 2.0

**Definition:** A permissive open-source license similar in spirit to MIT, but with added explicit protections around patents.

## How It Works
- Permits free use, modification, and distribution, similar to [[MIT License]]
- Includes an explicit patent grant: contributors license any patents they hold covering their contribution to users of the software
- Also includes a patent retaliation clause: if you sue someone over patents related to the software, your license to use it terminates

## Why It Matters
- The patent grant makes it a safer choice than MIT for large companies worried about patent litigation risk around open-source contributions

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming it's functionally identical to MIT, the patent clauses are a meaningful legal difference for companies with significant patent portfolios
