# Recession

**Definition:** A recession is a significant, widespread decline in economic activity that lasts more than a few months, typically reflected in falling output, employment, and income.

## How It Works
- Often identified by a sustained drop in GDP, alongside weakening indicators like rising unemployment and falling business activity
- Can be triggered by shocks such as tightening credit, falling demand, or external crises

## Why It Matters
- Recessions affect jobs, wages, and business investment, making them a central concern for policymakers and households alike
