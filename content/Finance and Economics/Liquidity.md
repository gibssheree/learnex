# Liquidity

**Definition:** Liquidity is how quickly and easily an asset can be converted into cash without significantly affecting its price.

## How It Works
- Highly liquid assets like cash or major stocks can be sold almost instantly at a known price; illiquid assets like real estate can take weeks or months to sell
- Markets with many buyers and sellers tend to be more liquid, since trades can happen quickly without moving the price much

## Why It Matters
- It affects how safely and quickly an investor can access cash in an emergency, and illiquid assets often carry extra risk during downturns
