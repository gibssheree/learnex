# Dividend

**Definition:** A dividend is a portion of a company's profits paid out to shareholders, usually in cash, on a regular schedule.

## How It Works
- Companies that generate steady profits may choose to distribute some of them to shareholders rather than reinvesting everything back into the business
- Payouts are typically declared per share and paid quarterly; not all companies pay dividends, especially fast-growing ones that reinvest earnings instead

## Why It Matters
- Dividends provide investors with income independent of stock price changes and, when reinvested, add to compounding returns over time

A company paying a $1 annual dividend per share gives an investor holding 100 shares $100 a year, regardless of the stock's price movement.
