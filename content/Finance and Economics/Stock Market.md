# Stock Market

**Definition:** The stock market is the collection of exchanges and marketplaces where shares of publicly traded companies are issued, bought, and sold.

## How It Works
- Companies list shares so investors can buy partial ownership; share prices move based on supply and demand driven by investor expectations
- Prices are influenced by company performance, broader economic conditions, and investor sentiment

## Why It Matters
- It's a primary way individuals and institutions invest and grow wealth over time, and a barometer of economic confidence

## Example
Buying a share of a public company makes an investor a partial owner, whose stake rises or falls in value with the stock price.
