# Mutual Funds and ETFs

**Definition:** Mutual funds and ETFs (exchange-traded funds) are pooled investment vehicles that let investors buy a diversified basket of stocks, bonds, or other assets through a single share.

## How It Works
- A fund manager or index pools money from many investors and buys a portfolio of securities on their behalf; each investor owns a proportional slice
- Mutual funds are priced once daily and bought directly from the fund company, while ETFs trade continuously on an exchange like a stock; both typically charge an annual expense ratio

## Why It Matters
- They give ordinary investors instant diversification and professional or index-based management without having to pick individual securities themselves

## Example
An investor buys shares of an S&P 500 index ETF, instantly gaining partial ownership in 500 companies instead of purchasing each stock separately.
