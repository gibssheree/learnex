# Inflation

**Definition:** Inflation is a general, sustained rise in the price level of goods and services over time, which erodes the purchasing power of money.

## How It Works
- Tracked using price indexes that follow the cost of a representative basket of goods and services over time
- Can be driven by rising demand outpacing supply, rising production costs, or growth in the money supply

## Why It Matters
- The same amount of money buys less over time, which affects wages, savings, and spending decisions

## Example
If inflation is running high, a fixed amount of savings held in cash will buy noticeably less a year later.
