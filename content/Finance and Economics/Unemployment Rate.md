# Unemployment Rate

**Definition:** The unemployment rate is the percentage of the labor force that is without a job and actively seeking work.

## How It Works
- It's calculated by dividing the number of unemployed people by the total labor force (employed plus unemployed job seekers)
- It typically rises during recessions as companies cut jobs, and falls as the economy grows and hiring picks up

## Why It Matters
- It's a key gauge of economic health that governments and central banks monitor when setting fiscal and monetary policy

## Example
When a country's unemployment rate rises from 4% to 8% during a downturn, it signals widespread job losses and weaker consumer spending.
