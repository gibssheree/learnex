# Market Capitalization

**Definition:** Market capitalization is the total value of a company's outstanding shares, calculated as share price multiplied by number of shares.

## How It Works
- It rises and falls automatically as the stock price moves, even if nothing about the company's operations has changed
- Companies are often grouped into large-, mid-, and small-cap categories, which investors use as a rough proxy for size, stability, and risk

## Why It Matters
- It's a quick way to compare company size and is widely used to build stock indexes and diversified portfolios

## Example
A company with 10 million shares trading at $50 each has a market capitalization of $500 million, placing it among mid-cap stocks.
