# Credit and Debt

**Definition:** Credit is the ability to borrow money with a promise to repay later, and debt is the amount owed as a result of borrowing.

## How It Works
- Lenders extend credit based on a borrower's perceived ability to repay, often judged by credit history and income; borrowed amounts accrue interest until repaid
- Good debt, like a loan for an appreciating asset, can build wealth, while high-interest debt can quickly compound against the borrower

## Why It Matters
- Managing credit and debt responsibly affects access to future loans, interest rates offered, and overall financial stability
