# Central Bank and Monetary Policy

**Definition:** A central bank is a country's monetary authority (such as the U.S. Federal Reserve), and monetary policy is its use of tools like interest rates and money supply to influence inflation and economic activity.

## How It Works
- By raising or lowering benchmark interest rates, a central bank makes borrowing more or less expensive, which cools or stimulates the economy
- It can also influence the money supply directly through other tools available to it

## Why It Matters
- Central bank decisions ripple through mortgage rates, loan costs, stock markets, and currency values
