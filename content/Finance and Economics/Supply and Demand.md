# Supply and Demand

**Definition:** Supply and demand is the basic economic model describing how the price of a good is set by the balance between how much of it is available and how much people want it.

## How It Works
- When demand rises relative to supply, prices tend to rise; when supply rises relative to demand, prices tend to fall
- The point where the quantity buyers want matches the quantity sellers offer is called equilibrium

## Why It Matters
- It explains why prices move the way they do in nearly every market, from groceries to stocks

## Example
A shortage of a popular product tends to push its price up until demand cools or more supply arrives.
