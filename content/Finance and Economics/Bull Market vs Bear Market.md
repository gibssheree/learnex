# Bull Market vs Bear Market

**Definition:** A bull market is a sustained period of rising asset prices, while a bear market is a sustained period of falling prices, typically a drop of 20% or more.

## How It Works
- Bull markets are driven by optimism, strong economic growth, and rising investor confidence, encouraging more buying
- Bear markets are driven by pessimism, economic weakness, or crises, often triggering selling and further price declines

## Why It Matters
- Recognizing which phase the market is in helps investors set expectations and avoid panic-selling during downturns or overconfidence during rallies

## Example
After the stock market fell over 20% during a economic crisis, analysts declared a bear market until prices later recovered into a new bull market.
