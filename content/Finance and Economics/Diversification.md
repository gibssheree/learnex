# Diversification

**Definition:** Diversification is the practice of spreading investments across different assets, sectors, or regions to reduce overall risk.

## How It Works
- Because different assets don't all move together, losses in one holding can be offset by stability or gains in another
- Achieved by holding a mix of asset types rather than concentrating money in a single investment

## Why It Matters
- It reduces the impact of any single investment performing badly, making a portfolio's overall returns more stable over time

## Example
Instead of putting all savings into one company's stock, an investor spreads money across many companies and asset types.
