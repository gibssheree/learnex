# Compound Interest

**Definition:** Compound interest is interest calculated on both the original principal and the interest that has already accumulated from prior periods.

## How It Works
- Each period's interest gets added to the balance, so future interest is earned on a growing base rather than just the original amount
- The longer money is left to grow, the more pronounced the compounding effect becomes

## Why It Matters
- It's the core mechanic behind long-term investing and why starting to save early has an outsized effect on final wealth

## Example
Reinvesting the dividends and gains from an investment each year, instead of withdrawing them, lets future returns compound on a larger balance.
