# Yield Curve

**Definition:** The yield curve is a line plotting interest rates on bonds of the same credit quality across different maturities, from short-term to long-term.

## How It Works
- Normally, longer-term bonds pay higher yields than short-term ones to compensate investors for locking up money longer
- When short-term yields exceed long-term yields, the curve "inverts," a pattern that has historically preceded recessions

## Why It Matters
- Investors and economists watch the yield curve's shape as a signal of market expectations for future growth and interest rates
