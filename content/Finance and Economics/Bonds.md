# Bonds

**Definition:** A bond is a loan an investor makes to a government or company in exchange for periodic interest payments and repayment of principal at maturity.

## How It Works
- The issuer borrows a fixed amount and agrees to pay a stated interest rate (coupon) over a set term, returning the principal when the bond matures
- Bond prices move opposite to interest rates: when rates rise, existing bonds with lower fixed payments become less attractive and fall in value

## Why It Matters
- Bonds offer more predictable income and lower volatility than stocks, making them a common tool for balancing risk in a portfolio
