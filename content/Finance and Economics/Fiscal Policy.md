# Fiscal Policy

**Definition:** Fiscal policy is a government's use of spending and taxation to influence economic activity.

## How It Works
- Governments increase spending or cut taxes to stimulate a slowing economy, and cut spending or raise taxes to cool an overheating one
- Expansionary policy tends to boost growth and jobs but can widen deficits; contractionary policy does the reverse

## Why It Matters
- It's one of the two main levers (alongside monetary policy) governments use to manage economic cycles and stabilize employment and prices

During a downturn, a government might pass a stimulus package that increases infrastructure spending to create jobs and boost demand.
