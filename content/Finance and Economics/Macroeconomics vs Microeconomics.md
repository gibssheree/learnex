# Macroeconomics vs Microeconomics

**Definition:** Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole (growth, inflation, unemployment), while microeconomics studies the decisions of individual households, firms, and markets.

## How It Works
- Macro looks at aggregates like national output and price levels; micro looks at supply, demand, and pricing for a single good or company
- The two are connected: millions of micro-level decisions add up to macro-level outcomes

## Why It Matters
- Choosing the right lens matters: a policy question about national interest rates is macro, while a question about why one company's product is priced a certain way is micro

## Example
A macroeconomist studies why national unemployment is rising, while a microeconomist studies why a specific factory is laying off workers.
