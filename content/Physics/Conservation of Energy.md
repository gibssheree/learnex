# Conservation of Energy

**Definition:** The principle that the total energy of an isolated system remains constant, changing only in form and never being created or destroyed.

## How It Works
- Energy converts between forms such as kinetic, potential, thermal, and chemical, but the total amount stays the same
- In real systems, energy often disperses as heat, which looks like a loss of "useful" energy even though the total is conserved

## Why It Matters
- It underlies the analysis of virtually every physical process, from simple machines to chemical reactions to planetary orbits

## Example
A swinging pendulum converts potential energy at its highest point into kinetic energy at its lowest point, and back again.
