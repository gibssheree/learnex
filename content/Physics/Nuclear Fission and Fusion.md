# Nuclear Fission and Fusion

**Definition:** Nuclear reactions that release energy by splitting heavy atomic nuclei apart (fission) or combining light nuclei together (fusion).

## How It Works
- Fission occurs when a heavy nucleus like uranium-235 absorbs a neutron and splits into smaller nuclei, releasing energy and additional neutrons that can sustain a chain reaction
- Fusion occurs when light nuclei like hydrogen isotopes combine under extreme heat and pressure to form a heavier nucleus, releasing even more energy per unit mass than fission

## Why It Matters
- Fission powers nuclear reactors and weapons, while fusion powers stars and is being pursued as a clean, high-yield energy source for the future

## Example
The Sun generates its energy by fusing hydrogen nuclei into helium in its core, a process scientists are trying to replicate on Earth for power generation.
