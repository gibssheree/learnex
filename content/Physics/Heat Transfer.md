# Heat Transfer

**Definition:** The movement of thermal energy from a hotter object or region to a cooler one, occurring through conduction, convection, or radiation.

## How It Works
- Conduction transfers heat through direct contact as fast-moving particles collide with slower ones; convection transfers heat through the bulk movement of fluids like air or water
- Radiation transfers heat as electromagnetic waves and can travel through empty space, unlike the other two mechanisms

## Why It Matters
- Understanding heat transfer is essential for designing insulation, engines, cooling systems, and climate models, since it governs how energy moves through everyday and industrial systems

## Example
A metal spoon left in hot soup becomes warm at its handle through conduction, even though only its tip is touching the liquid.
