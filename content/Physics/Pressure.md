# Pressure

**Definition:** The amount of force applied perpendicular to a surface per unit area, commonly experienced in fluids, gases, and solids.

## How It Works
- Pressure equals force divided by area (P = F/A), so the same force spread over a smaller area produces much higher pressure
- In fluids, pressure increases with depth and acts equally in all directions, a principle described by Pascal's law and used in hydraulic systems

## Why It Matters
- Pressure concepts underlie weather systems, hydraulics, breathing, and structural engineering, making it one of the most widely applied quantities in physics

## Example
A sharp knife cuts more easily than a blunt one because its thin edge concentrates the same applied force onto a much smaller area, producing higher pressure.
