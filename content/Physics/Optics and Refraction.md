# Optics and Refraction

**Definition:** Optics is the study of light's behavior, and refraction is the bending of light as it passes between materials of different density.

## How It Works
- Light bends at the boundary between two media because it changes speed, with the amount of bending described by Snell's law
- Denser materials like glass or water slow light more than air, bending it toward the normal line, which lenses and prisms exploit to focus or split light

## Why It Matters
- Refraction principles enable eyeglasses, cameras, microscopes, and fiber-optic communication, making it central to both vision correction and modern technology

## Example
A straw appears bent when placed in a glass of water, because light rays refract as they pass from water into air on their way to your eye.
