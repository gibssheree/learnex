# Thermodynamics

**Definition:** The branch of physics that studies heat, work, temperature, and the transfer of energy between systems.

## How It Works
- The first law states energy is conserved: heat added to a system equals its change in internal energy plus the work it does
- The second law states that entropy in an isolated system never decreases, which sets the direction of spontaneous processes and limits efficiency

## Why It Matters
- It governs the design of engines, refrigerators, and power plants, and explains why many real processes are irreversible

## Example
A car engine converts heat from burning fuel into mechanical work but always releases some heat as waste, per the second law.
