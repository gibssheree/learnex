# Kinetic and Potential Energy

**Definition:** The two basic forms of mechanical energy: kinetic energy from motion and potential energy stored due to position or configuration.

## How It Works
- Kinetic energy depends on mass and velocity (KE = ½mv²), growing rapidly as speed increases
- Potential energy depends on position within a force field, such as gravitational height or a compressed spring; the two convert into each other as an object moves

## Why It Matters
- Understanding this interplay lets engineers design roller coasters, pendulums, and energy storage systems, and explains why falling objects speed up

## Example
A skier at the top of a hill has maximum potential energy; as they descend, that potential energy converts into kinetic energy, reaching maximum speed at the bottom.
