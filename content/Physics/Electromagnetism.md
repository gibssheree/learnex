# Electromagnetism

**Definition:** The fundamental force of nature that governs interactions between electrically charged particles, unifying electricity and magnetism into a single phenomenon.

## How It Works
- Electric charges create electric fields, and moving charges (currents) create magnetic fields; a changing magnetic field induces an electric field, and vice versa
- Light itself is an electromagnetic wave: a self-propagating oscillation of electric and magnetic fields

## Why It Matters
- It powers nearly all modern technology, including electric motors, generators, radio, and electronics
