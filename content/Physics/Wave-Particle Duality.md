# Wave-Particle Duality

**Definition:** The quantum principle that light and matter exhibit both wave-like and particle-like properties depending on how they are observed.

## How It Works
- Light behaves as a wave in phenomena like interference and diffraction, but as discrete particles (photons) in phenomena like the photoelectric effect
- Matter particles such as electrons can likewise show wave-like interference patterns, as demonstrated in the double-slit experiment

## Why It Matters
- It reveals that classical concepts of "wave" and "particle" are insufficient at the quantum scale, motivating the development of quantum mechanics

## Example
In the double-slit experiment, individual electrons build up an interference pattern over time, as if each one behaves like a wave.
