# Entropy

**Definition:** A measure of the disorder, randomness, or number of possible microscopic arrangements in a system, which tends to increase over time in an isolated system.

## How It Works
- The second law of thermodynamics states that the total entropy of an isolated system never decreases, driving processes toward greater disorder
- Higher entropy corresponds to more ways a system's particles can be arranged while still looking the same at the macroscopic level

## Why It Matters
- It explains why many everyday processes are irreversible, such as heat flowing from hot to cold and not the reverse
