# Electric Field and Magnetic Field

**Definition:** Regions of space around charged particles or magnets where electric or magnetic forces act on other charges or magnetic materials.

## How It Works
- An electric field radiates outward from a charge and exerts force on other charges, growing stronger with charge magnitude and weaker with distance
- A magnetic field surrounds moving charges or magnets and exerts force only on other moving charges or magnetic materials; a changing one of either field generates the other

## Why It Matters
- Together they explain electric motors, generators, and electromagnetic radiation, forming the basis of nearly all modern electrical technology
