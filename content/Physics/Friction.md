# Friction

**Definition:** A resistive force that opposes relative motion or the tendency of motion between two surfaces in contact.

## How It Works
- Friction arises from microscopic irregularities and molecular interactions between surfaces, and its magnitude depends on the normal force pressing them together and a material-specific friction coefficient
- Static friction resists the start of motion and is generally stronger than kinetic friction, which acts once surfaces are already sliding

## Why It Matters
- Friction is essential for walking, driving, and gripping objects, but it also wastes energy as heat in machines, making its control a key engineering concern

## Example
Rubbing your hands together quickly generates heat because friction converts the mechanical energy of motion into thermal energy.
