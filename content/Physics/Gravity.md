# Gravity

**Definition:** The fundamental force of attraction between any two masses, responsible for weight on Earth and the large-scale structure of the universe.

## How It Works
- Newton's law of universal gravitation states that attractive force grows with the product of two masses and shrinks with the square of the distance between them
- Einstein's general relativity reframes gravity as the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy, which better explains extreme cases like black holes

## Why It Matters
- Gravity governs planetary orbits, tides, and the trajectories of spacecraft, making it essential to astronomy, satellite engineering, and everyday concepts like weight

## Example
The Moon continuously falls toward Earth under gravity, but its sideways velocity keeps it in a stable orbit rather than crashing down.
