# Sound and Acoustics

**Definition:** Sound is a mechanical wave produced by vibrating objects that travels through a medium as pressure variations; acoustics is the study of how sound is generated, propagated, and perceived.

## How It Works
- Sound waves are longitudinal, meaning particles of the medium vibrate parallel to the direction of travel, compressing and rarefying as the wave passes
- A sound's pitch corresponds to its frequency and its loudness to its amplitude, while the medium's density and elasticity determine how fast it travels

## Why It Matters
- Understanding acoustics enables the design of concert halls, noise-cancelling technology, medical ultrasound imaging, and sonar for navigation and detection

## Example
A guitar string's vibration compresses and expands the surrounding air in a repeating pattern, and these pressure waves travel to a listener's ear as an audible tone.
