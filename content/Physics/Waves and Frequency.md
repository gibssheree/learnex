# Waves and Frequency

**Definition:** A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy through space or a medium, and frequency is the number of oscillations it completes per second.

## How It Works
- Waves are characterized by wavelength, amplitude, and frequency, related by the equation speed = wavelength × frequency
- Higher frequency waves carry more energy per oscillation, and frequency stays constant when a wave passes between media even as its speed and wavelength change

## Why It Matters
- Wave behavior underlies sound, light, radio communication, and quantum phenomena, making frequency a key variable across acoustics, optics, and electronics

A guitar string plucked to vibrate faster produces a higher-pitched note, because increasing frequency raises the perceived sound pitch.
