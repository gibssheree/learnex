# Quantum Mechanics

**Definition:** The branch of physics describing the behavior of matter and energy at atomic and subatomic scales, where outcomes are fundamentally probabilistic rather than deterministic.

## How It Works
- Particles are described by a wavefunction that gives the probability of finding them in a particular state or location, rather than a definite trajectory
- Quantities like energy and angular momentum are often quantized, taking only discrete allowed values

## Why It Matters
- It underlies modern technologies like semiconductors, lasers, and MRI machines, and explains atomic structure and chemical bonding

## Example
An electron in an atom can only occupy specific discrete energy levels, not any arbitrary value in between.
