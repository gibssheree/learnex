# Polymers

**Definition:** A polymer is a large molecule made of many repeating structural units, called monomers, that are chemically bonded together into long chains or networks.

## How It Works
- Monomers link through polymerization reactions, either by addition (monomers joining without byproducts) or condensation (monomers joining while releasing a small molecule like water)
- The arrangement, length, and cross-linking of polymer chains determine bulk properties like flexibility, strength, and melting behavior

## Why It Matters
- Polymers make up plastics, rubber, textiles, and biological molecules like DNA and proteins, making them central to both industrial materials and life itself

## Example
Polyethylene, used in plastic bags and bottles, forms when many small ethylene monomers link together through addition polymerization into a long carbon chain.
