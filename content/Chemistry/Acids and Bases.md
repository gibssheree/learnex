# Acids and Bases

**Definition:** Acids are substances that donate hydrogen ions (H+) in solution, while bases accept them or donate hydroxide ions (OH-), and the pH scale measures how acidic or basic a solution is on a range from 0 to 14.

## How It Works
- A pH of 7 is neutral (pure water), below 7 is acidic, and above 7 is basic; each whole-number step represents a tenfold change in H+ concentration
- Acids and bases can neutralize each other, reacting to form water and a salt

## Why It Matters
- pH governs whether chemical and biological processes can happen at all, from digestion to industrial manufacturing

## Example
Lemon juice has a pH around 2 (acidic), while baking soda dissolved in water has a pH around 9 (basic).
