# Chemical Bonding

**Definition:** Chemical bonding is the attraction that holds atoms together in molecules or compounds, primarily through ionic bonds (electron transfer) or covalent bonds (electron sharing).

## How It Works
- Ionic bonds form when one atom transfers electrons to another, creating oppositely charged ions that attract each other (typically metal + nonmetal)
- Covalent bonds form when atoms share electron pairs, usually between nonmetals, to each achieve a more stable outer electron shell

## Why It Matters
- The type of bond largely determines a substance's physical properties, like melting point, conductivity, and solubility

## Example
Table salt (NaCl) forms an ionic bond between sodium and chlorine, while water (H2O) forms covalent bonds between oxygen and hydrogen.
