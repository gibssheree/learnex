# Reaction Kinetics

**Definition:** Reaction kinetics is the study of the rates at which chemical reactions proceed and the factors that influence how fast reactants convert into products.

## How It Works
- Reaction rate depends on factors like concentration, temperature, surface area, and the presence of a catalyst, all of which affect how often and how energetically molecules collide
- Rate laws express how rate depends mathematically on reactant concentrations, while activation energy describes the energy barrier molecules must overcome to react

## Why It Matters
- Controlling reaction speed is essential for everything from designing safe industrial processes to formulating drugs that release at the right rate in the body

## Example
Food spoils faster at room temperature than in a refrigerator because lower temperatures slow the reaction rates of the chemical processes causing decay.
