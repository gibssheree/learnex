# Biochemistry Basics

**Definition:** Biochemistry is the study of the chemical processes and molecules, such as proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, and nucleic acids, that occur within living organisms.

## How It Works
- Large biomolecules are built from repeating monomer units linked by specific bonds, such as amino acids forming proteins or nucleotides forming DNA and RNA
- Enzymes, which are typically proteins, catalyze the reactions that build and break down these molecules, allowing cells to extract energy and construct the structures they need

## Why It Matters
- Biochemistry underlies medicine, nutrition, and biotechnology, explaining everything from how drugs interact with the body to how genetic information is copied and expressed

## Example
Digestive enzymes like amylase break the glycosidic bonds in starch, converting it into glucose molecules the body can absorb and use for energy.
