# Organic Chemistry Basics

**Definition:** Organic chemistry is the study of carbon-containing compounds, focusing on their structure, properties, and the reactions that transform them.

## How It Works
- Carbon's ability to form four stable bonds, including with itself, allows it to build long chains, branches, and rings that create enormous molecular diversity
- Molecules with the same formula but different structures, called isomers, can behave very differently, so structure determines chemical and physical properties

## Why It Matters
- Organic chemistry is the foundation of biochemistry, pharmaceuticals, plastics, and fuels, since nearly all molecules of life and most synthetic materials are carbon-based

## Example
Ethanol and dimethyl ether share the formula C2H6O but have different structures, giving ethanol a hydroxyl group that makes it a liquid alcohol rather than a gas.
