# Molarity and Solutions

**Definition:** Molarity is a measure of concentration expressed as the number of moles of solute dissolved per liter of solution.

## How It Works
- A solution is made of a solute (the substance being dissolved) dissolved in a solvent (the substance doing the dissolving)
- Molarity is calculated as moles of solute divided by liters of solution, written as mol/L or M

## Why It Matters
- Precise concentration measurements are essential for reliably preparing solutions in labs, medicine, and manufacturing

## Example
Dissolving 1 mole of table salt in enough water to make 1 liter of solution produces a 1 M (one molar) saltwater solution.
