# Functional Groups

**Definition:** A functional group is a specific cluster of atoms within a molecule that determines its characteristic chemical reactions and properties, regardless of the rest of the molecule's structure.

## How It Works
- Common functional groups include hydroxyl (-OH), carboxyl (-COOH), amine (-NH2), and carbonyl (C=O), each giving molecules containing them predictable reactivity
- Because functional groups react in consistent ways, chemists can predict how an unfamiliar molecule will behave just by identifying the groups it contains

## Why It Matters
- Recognizing functional groups lets chemists classify organic compounds, design drugs with specific effects, and understand why molecules like sugars or fats behave so differently
