# Chemical Reactions and Stoichiometry

**Definition:** A chemical reaction is a process where reactants transform into new substances called products, and stoichiometry is the calculation of the quantitative relationships between them using balanced equations.

## How It Works
- A balanced equation has equal numbers of each type of atom on both sides, reflecting the law of conservation of mass
- Coefficients in the balanced equation give the mole ratios used to calculate how much of each reactant or product is involved

## Why It Matters
- Stoichiometry lets chemists predict exactly how much product a reaction will yield or how much reactant is needed, which is essential for lab work and manufacturing

## Example
The balanced equation 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O shows that two molecules of hydrogen react with one molecule of oxygen to form two molecules of water.
