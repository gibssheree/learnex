# Titration

**Definition:** Titration is a laboratory technique for determining the concentration of an unknown solution by slowly reacting it with a solution of known concentration until the reaction reaches its endpoint.

## How It Works
- A titrant of known concentration is added dropwise from a burette to a measured volume of analyte until an indicator changes color or a pH meter signals the equivalence point
- The volume of titrant used, combined with its known concentration, lets chemists calculate the moles and therefore the concentration of the unknown solution via stoichiometry

## Why It Matters
- Titration underlies quality control in pharmaceuticals, food testing, water analysis, and any setting where precise concentration measurements are required
