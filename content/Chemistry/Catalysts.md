# Catalysts

**Definition:** A catalyst is a substance that speeds up the rate of a chemical reaction by lowering its activation energy, without being consumed or permanently changed in the process.

## How It Works
- Catalysts provide an alternative reaction pathway that requires less energy to get started, so more reactant molecules can react in a given time
- Because the catalyst is regenerated at the end of the reaction, only a small amount is needed to keep working repeatedly

## Why It Matters
- Catalysts make otherwise slow or impractical reactions economically viable, from industrial chemical production to the enzymes that drive reactions in living cells

## Example
The catalytic converter in a car uses platinum and palladium to speed up the conversion of toxic exhaust gases into less harmful ones.
