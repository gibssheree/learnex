# Kirchhoff's Laws

**Definition:** Kirchhoff's Laws are two rules describing how current and voltage behave in electrical circuits: the Current Law (KCL) and the Voltage Law (KVL).

## How It Works
- KCL states that the total current flowing into a junction equals the total current flowing out, since charge can't accumulate at a node
- KVL states that the sum of all voltage drops around any closed loop equals the total voltage supplied; together they let you solve for unknown currents and voltages in complex networks

## Why It Matters
- They're the foundation for systematic circuit analysis, letting engineers solve networks too complex for Ohm's Law alone
