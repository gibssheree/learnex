# Electric Motors

**Definition:** An electric motor is a device that converts electrical energy into mechanical rotation using magnetic fields.

## How It Works
- Current flowing through coils inside the motor creates a magnetic field that interacts with permanent magnets or other coils, producing a force that turns the rotor; commutators or electronic controllers keep switching current direction so the rotation continues
- DC motors, AC induction motors, and brushless motors differ mainly in how they generate and time this rotating magnetic field

## Why It Matters
- Motors power everything from tiny vibration units in phones to industrial pumps and electric vehicles, making them one of the most common ways electrical energy becomes useful work
