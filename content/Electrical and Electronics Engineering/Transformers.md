# Transformers

**Definition:** A transformer is a device that transfers electrical energy between circuits by electromagnetic induction, typically changing voltage level in the process.

## How It Works
- Two or more coils of wire (windings) share a common magnetic core; a changing AC current in the primary winding induces a voltage in the secondary winding
- The voltage ratio between windings matches their turns ratio, so more turns on the secondary steps voltage up, and fewer turns steps it down; power (minus small losses) stays roughly constant, so current changes inversely to voltage

## Why It Matters
- Transformers let power grids transmit electricity at very high voltage and low current to cut resistive losses, then step voltage back down to safe levels near homes and devices

## Example
A utility substation transformer steps 138 kV transmission voltage down to 12 kV for local distribution, and a small transformer inside a wall adapter steps it down further to charge electronics.
