# Transistor

**Definition:** A transistor is a semiconductor device that can amplify or switch electronic signals, using a small input to control a much larger current flow.

## How It Works
- It has three terminals; a small voltage or current at one terminal controls the current flow between the other two
- Used as a switch, it toggles between fully "on" and fully "off" states, which is how digital logic gates are built

## Why It Matters
- Transistors are the fundamental building block of digital logic, memory, and amplification, billions of them make up a modern processor
