# AC vs DC

**Definition:** AC (alternating current) periodically reverses direction and magnitude, while DC (direct current) flows steadily in one direction.

## How It Works
- AC typically follows a sine wave oscillating at a fixed frequency, such as 50/60 Hz for mains power; DC stays at a constant polarity, as from a battery
- Transformers can efficiently step AC voltage up or down, which is part of why power grids distribute power as AC

## Why It Matters
- Almost every electronic device runs on DC internally, so power supplies exist specifically to convert AC mains into DC
