# Signal Modulation

**Definition:** Modulation is the process of varying a carrier wave's amplitude, frequency, or phase to encode information onto it for transmission.

## How It Works
- Amplitude modulation (AM) varies the carrier's strength, frequency modulation (FM) varies its frequency, and phase modulation shifts its timing, each tracing the shape of the original signal
- The modulated signal travels efficiently over long distances or through the air, then a receiver demodulates it to recover the original information

## Why It Matters
- Modulation makes radio, television, WiFi, and cellular communication possible by letting many signals share the airwaves without interfering
