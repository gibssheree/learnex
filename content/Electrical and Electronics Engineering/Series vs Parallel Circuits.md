# Series vs Parallel Circuits

**Definition:** In a series circuit, components are connected end-to-end along a single path; in a parallel circuit, components are connected across shared nodes on multiple paths.

## How It Works
- Series: the same current flows through every component, and the supply voltage divides across them
- Parallel: the same voltage appears across every branch, and the total current divides based on each branch's resistance

## Why It Matters
- The wiring choice determines what happens if one component fails: a broken series link stops the whole circuit, while parallel branches keep working independently

## Example
Old-style string lights wired in series all go dark if one bulb burns out; household outlets are wired in parallel so one dead lamp doesn't affect the others.
