# Ohm's Law

**Definition:** Ohm's Law states that the voltage across a conductor equals the current through it multiplied by its resistance: V = I × R.

## How It Works
- Rearranged, I = V / R and R = V / I let you solve for whichever quantity is unknown
- It holds for "ohmic" components like resistors, but not for components such as diodes whose resistance changes with voltage

## Why It Matters
- It's the single most-used equation for sizing resistors, predicting current draw, and debugging circuits
