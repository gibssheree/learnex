# Integrated Circuit

**Definition:** An integrated circuit (IC), or chip, is a complete electronic circuit, often with millions of transistors, fabricated onto a single small piece of semiconductor material.

## How It Works
- Layers of doped silicon, metal, and insulator are etched using photolithography to build interconnected transistors, resistors, and diodes at microscopic scale
- The finished die is packaged with external pins so it can be soldered onto a printed circuit board like a single component

## Why It Matters
- ICs made complex electronics dramatically smaller, cheaper, and more reliable than circuits built from discrete components

## Example
A single op-amp IC the size of a fingernail replaces what would otherwise require dozens of individually wired transistors and resistors.
