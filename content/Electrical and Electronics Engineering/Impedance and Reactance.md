# Impedance and Reactance

**Definition:** Impedance is the total opposition a circuit presents to alternating current, combining resistance with reactance from capacitors and inductors.

## How It Works
- Reactance is the frequency-dependent opposition from capacitors and inductors; capacitive reactance falls as frequency rises, while inductive reactance rises with it
- Impedance combines resistance and reactance as a complex quantity, affecting both the amplitude and phase of AC signals

## Why It Matters
- Understanding impedance is essential for designing filters, matching audio/RF systems, and predicting how circuits behave at different frequencies
