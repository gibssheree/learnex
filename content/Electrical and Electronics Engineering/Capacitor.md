# Capacitor

**Definition:** A capacitor is a component that stores electrical energy in an electric field between two conductive plates separated by an insulator.

## How It Works
- Its capacitance, measured in farads, determines how much charge it can store per volt applied across it
- It resists sudden changes in voltage, charging and discharging over time rather than instantly

## Why It Matters
- Capacitors are used for filtering noise from power supplies, timing circuits, and short-term energy storage

## Example
A capacitor placed across a power supply smooths out voltage ripples before the current reaches sensitive components.
