# Voltage, Current, and Resistance

**Definition:** Voltage is the electrical potential difference that pushes charge through a circuit, current is the rate of flow of that charge, and resistance is the opposition a material offers to that flow.

## How It Works
- Voltage (V, volts) is measured across two points, current (I, amps) is measured through a path, and resistance (R, ohms) is a property of the conductor or component
- The three are linked directly: raise resistance and current drops for the same applied voltage

## Why It Matters
- Nearly every circuit calculation and component rating starts from these three quantities
