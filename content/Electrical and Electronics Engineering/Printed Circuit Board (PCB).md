# Printed Circuit Board (PCB)

**Definition:** A printed circuit board is a flat board of insulating material with conductive copper traces etched onto it that mechanically supports and electrically connects components.

## How It Works
- Copper traces are etched or printed onto the board to form the wiring pattern, replacing loose hand-soldered wires
- Components are soldered onto pads connected to these traces; multi-layer boards stack several trace layers to route dense circuits

## Why It Matters
- PCBs enable reliable, compact, mass-producible electronics by standardizing how components connect

## Example
A smartphone's motherboard is a multi-layer PCB routing thousands of connections between its processor, memory, and other chips in a space smaller than a credit card.
