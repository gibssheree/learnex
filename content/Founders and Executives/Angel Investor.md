# Angel Investor

**Definition:** A wealthy individual who invests their own personal money into early-stage startups, usually in exchange for equity or a convertible instrument.

## How It Works
- Angels typically write smaller checks than venture capital firms and invest earlier, often before a startup has significant traction or even a finished product
- They may invest solo or pool capital through angel groups or syndicates, and frequently offer mentorship, industry connections, or advisory help alongside their money

## Why It Matters
- Fills the funding gap between founders' own savings and institutional venture capital, letting early startups get off the ground before they qualify for larger rounds
