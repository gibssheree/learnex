# Convertible Note

**Definition:** A short-term loan from investors to a startup that converts into equity at a future financing round, usually at a discount or capped valuation.

## How It Works
- Investors lend cash now and receive shares later instead of negotiating a valuation immediately; the note accrues interest until it converts
- A "cap" sets the maximum valuation used for conversion, protecting early investors if the company's value rises sharply before the next round

## Why It Matters
- Lets founders raise money quickly without the time and legal cost of pricing the company, which is especially useful at the earliest stage

## Example
A startup raises $300K via convertible notes with a $6M cap; when the Series A prices the company at $10M, note holders convert as if the valuation were $6M.
