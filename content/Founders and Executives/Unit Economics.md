# Unit Economics

**Definition:** The direct revenues and costs associated with a single customer or unit of a business, most commonly compared as customer lifetime value (LTV) against customer acquisition cost (CAC).

## How It Works
- LTV estimates the total value a customer generates over their relationship with the business
- CAC measures what it costs, in sales and marketing, to acquire one new customer

## Why It Matters
- A healthy LTV-to-CAC relationship signals a business can grow profitably, not just grow
