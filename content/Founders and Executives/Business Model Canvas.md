# Business Model Canvas

**Definition:** A one-page strategic template that maps out how a business creates, delivers, and captures value across key building blocks like customers, value proposition, channels, and revenue.

## How It Works
- Organizes a business into blocks such as customer segments, value propositions, channels, revenue streams, and cost structure
- Encourages founders to see the whole business at a glance and spot gaps or mismatches

## Why It Matters
- Gives a fast, shared way to sketch and stress-test a business idea before writing a full business plan

A founder filling out a Business Model Canvas in an afternoon to clarify who the customer actually is before building anything.
