# MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

**Definition:** The smallest version of a product that lets a team test their core idea with real users while investing minimal time and resources.

## How It Works
- Cut every feature that isn't required to test the core hypothesis
- Ship it to real users and measure how they actually respond

## Why It Matters
- Lets founders learn and iterate before over-investing in a direction that might not work

- [[Product-Market Fit]]

## Example
A founder testing a meal-planning app idea by manually sending users a weekly PDF plan instead of building the full app.
