# SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)

**Definition:** An investment instrument, created by Y Combinator, in which an investor gives a startup cash now in exchange for the right to receive equity at a future priced round, rather than a loan.

## How It Works
- Converts into equity automatically when the startup later raises a priced round, typically at a discount or valuation cap
- Skips setting a fixed valuation at the time of investment, unlike a traditional priced equity round

## Why It Matters
- Lets early-stage startups raise money quickly and cheaply without negotiating a full valuation upfront
