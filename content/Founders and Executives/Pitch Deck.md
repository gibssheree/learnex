# Pitch Deck

**Definition:** The slide deck founders use to present their startup's problem, solution, market, and traction to potential investors.

## How It Works
- Typically walks through the problem, solution, market size, business model, traction, team, and funding ask
- Used both to secure investor meetings and to guide the conversation once in the room

## Why It Matters
- Often the first real impression an investor forms of a startup, so clarity and a strong narrative matter as much as the underlying facts
