# Venture Capital

**Definition:** Professional investors who provide capital to startups with high growth potential in exchange for equity ownership.

## How It Works
- Firms raise a fund from limited partners and invest it across a portfolio of startups, expecting most to fail and a few to return the fund many times over
- Typically invest in stages, from seed through later rounds, often taking a board seat alongside their investment

## Why It Matters
- Gives fast-growing startups access to capital, and often expertise and networks, beyond what founders could raise personally
