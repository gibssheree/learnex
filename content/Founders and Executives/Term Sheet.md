# Term Sheet

**Definition:** A short, mostly non-binding document that outlines the proposed key terms of an investment before the parties draft the final legal agreements.

## How It Works
- Covers core deal points like valuation, investment amount, equity type, and investor rights
- Signals serious intent to invest and sets the framework that lawyers then turn into binding contracts

## Why It Matters
- Lets founders and investors agree on the big-picture deal before spending time and money on full legal documentation

## Example
An investor sending a founder a term sheet proposing a specific valuation and board seat ahead of finalizing the round.
