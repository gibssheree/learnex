# Co-Founder Equity Split

**Definition:** The agreed division of company ownership among the founding team, decided early and typically formalized before outside funding.

## How It Works
- Founders negotiate splits based on factors like idea origin, time commitment, prior experience, and capital contributed, rather than defaulting to an even split
- Ownership is usually granted subject to [[Vesting and Cliff]] schedules so a founder who leaves early does not keep a large stake they didn't earn

## Why It Matters
- Getting this wrong causes some of the most damaging startup conflicts; a clear, fair split early on prevents resentment and legal disputes as the company grows

## Related Terms
- [[Vesting and Cliff]]
- [[Dilution]]
- [[Board of Directors]]

## Example
Two co-founders agree to a 60/40 split reflecting that one originated the idea and has worked on it full-time for a year longer than the other.
