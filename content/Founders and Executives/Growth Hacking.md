# Growth Hacking

**Definition:** A resourceful, experiment-driven approach to rapid user acquisition that relies on creativity, analytics, and low-cost tactics instead of large marketing budgets.

## How It Works
- Teams run fast, cheap experiments across product, marketing, and virality loops, then double down on whatever moves the growth metric that matters
- Often blends product features (referral incentives, viral sharing, onboarding tweaks) with marketing channels; success is measured relentlessly against a single key metric rather than intuition

## Why It Matters
- Lets cash-constrained startups compete for users against well-funded rivals by substituting speed and ingenuity for ad spend

A startup adds a "refer a friend, both get a free month" mechanic that drives most of its new signups without paying for ads.
