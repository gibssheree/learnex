# Vesting and Cliff

**Definition:** Vesting is the schedule by which founders or employees gradually earn ownership of their equity over time, and a cliff is the initial period before any of it vests.

## How It Works
- A common structure vests equity over several years, with a one-year cliff before the first portion is earned
- Leaving before the cliff typically means forfeiting all unvested equity

## Why It Matters
- Protects the company and co-founders by ensuring equity is earned through continued contribution, not handed out upfront
