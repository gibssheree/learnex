# ESOP (Employee Stock Option Pool)

**Definition:** A block of company equity set aside specifically to grant stock options to employees as part of their compensation.

## How It Works
- Investors typically require the pool be carved out of the [[Cap Table]] before a priced round, so its dilution falls mainly on existing shareholders rather than new investors
- Individual grants usually follow standard [[Vesting and Cliff]] schedules, giving employees ownership that vests gradually over their tenure

## Why It Matters
- Lets startups attract talent that cash compensation alone couldn't, aligning employee incentives with long-term company growth and a successful [[Exit Strategy]]

## Related Terms
- [[Vesting and Cliff]]
- [[Dilution]]
- [[Cap Table]]

## Example
Before a Series A, a startup expands its option pool to 15% of shares so it can offer competitive equity grants to the engineers it plans to hire.
