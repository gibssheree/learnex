# Cap Table

**Definition:** A record, typically a spreadsheet, that shows who owns what percentage of a company across founders, investors, and employees.

## How It Works
- Lists each shareholder along with their number of shares, share class, and resulting ownership percentage
- Gets updated every time new shares are issued, such as after a funding round or option grant

## Why It Matters
- Keeps everyone aligned on ownership and dilution, which matters heavily during fundraising and any eventual exit
