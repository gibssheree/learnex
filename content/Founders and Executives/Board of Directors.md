# Board of Directors

**Definition:** The group of individuals, elected by shareholders, that oversees a company's major decisions and holds fiduciary responsibility for its direction.

## How It Works
- Typically includes founders, investor representatives from major funding rounds, and independent members; composition often shifts as new investors gain board seats
- Approves major actions like fundraising, executive hires, and acquisitions, and formally votes on items requiring legal sign-off

## Why It Matters
- A well-composed board provides strategic guidance and credibility, but founders must manage board dynamics carefully since investors often gain real control through seats granted in a [[Term Sheet]]

## Example
After a Series A, a startup's board grows from two founders to five members, adding the lead investor's partner and an independent industry expert.
