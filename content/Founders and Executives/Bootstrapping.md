# Bootstrapping

**Definition:** Building and growing a company using personal savings and business revenue instead of outside investment.

## How It Works
- Founders fund early operations from their own money or early customer revenue, reinvesting profits to grow rather than raising rounds from investors
- Forces tight discipline around [[Runway and Burn Rate]] since there is no investor cash cushion to fall back on if growth stalls

## Why It Matters
- Preserves full ownership and decision-making control, avoiding the [[Dilution]] and investor expectations that come with venture funding, though it typically means slower growth

## Related Terms
- [[Runway and Burn Rate]]
- [[Dilution]]
- [[Venture Capital]]

## Example
A founder builds a profitable software tool using savings and early customer payments, reaching $1M in annual revenue without ever raising a funding round.
